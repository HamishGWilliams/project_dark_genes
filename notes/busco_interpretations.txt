Interpretation:

95.6% complete is very good. Your proteome is broadly complete.
2.6% fragmented and 1.8% missing are low enough that the dataset is usable.
The main caution is 42.7% duplicated BUSCOs, which is high.

That high duplication does not stop the project, but it means we should be careful. It can reflect one or more of:

retained redundant models
isoforms/transcript redundancy not fully collapsed
haplotig/assembly redundancy
overprediction in the annotation set

So from here onward, any dark-gene calls need to be made conservatively, especially for:

short proteins
singletons
proteins on suspiciously repetitive or duplicated regions
proteins with weak structural support
